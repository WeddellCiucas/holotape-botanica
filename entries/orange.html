ORANGE
──────────────────────────── 


⸙ It used to be a rather small evergreen flowering tree that could grow up to ten meters.
⸙ Now it's been adapted to grow in Vault hydroponic bays, the average height not reaching more than a standard shrub.
⸙ The leaves are oval and green with crenulate margins. 
⸙ The fruits are formed as tightly packed segments, juicy and sweet, covered with a thick leather-like peel. 
⸙ The color ranges from dull to vibrant orange.
⸙ The fruits are packed with sugars and vintamin C, a good countermeassure against wasteland scurvy and similar diseases.
