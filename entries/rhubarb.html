────────────────────────────
RHUBARB
──────────────────────────── 


⸙ Hardy, leafy perennial plant occasionally cultivated in subterranean vaults and agricultural wasteland settlements.
⸙ The large, triangular leaves contain high levels of oxalic acid and anthrone glycosides, making them poisonous and therefore inedible. 
⸙ They do not cause death or severe symptoms in low ammounts, thankfully.
⸙ The leaf stalks can be used raw while they have a crisp texture, but are most commonly cooked with sugar and used in pies, crumbles, and other desserts.
⸙ They have a strong, tart taste.
