PLUTEUS UMBROSUS
──────────────────────────── 


𓍊𓋼 Pluteus umbrosus is a fairly rare late summer and autumn fungus that thrives on rotting wood and damp cave environments.
𓍊𓋼 Also known as the Velvet Shield, the cap measures 3 to 9 cm in diameter and is velvety brown, wrinkly in appearance. The gills are pinkish with a dark brown squamulous edge. The stem is whitish to pale brown, with dark squamules and grows from 3 to 8 cm long and 5 to 12 mm thick.
𓍊𓋼 Culinarily, it's generally described as having a mild, indistinct, or slightly bitter taste, sometimes accompanied by an odor resembling radish or raw potatoes.
𓍊𓋼 It's not toxic, but it's not sought-after either.
