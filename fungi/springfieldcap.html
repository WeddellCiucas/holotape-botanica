SPRING FIELDCAP 
──────────────────────────── 


𓍊𓋼 Agrocybe praecox or the Spring Fieldcap is a species of fungi that typically grow in forests, but it can also sprout in old mines and caves on decaying wood fibers.
𓍊𓋼 The cap is usually up to 4-10 centimeters wide, smooth, and tannish-yellow. The stem is pale, 4 to 12 cm long and about 2 cm thick. The gills are initially pale grayish, later brown.
𓍊𓋼 The mushroom itself is not inedible if cooked well, but popular opinion classifies them as better left aside, unless there's nothing better to scavenge for.
𓍊𓋼 The and smell has a floury profile. 
𓍊𓋼 If untreated, it can present radioisotope compounds.
