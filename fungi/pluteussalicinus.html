PLUTEUS SALICINUS
──────────────────────────── 


𓍊𓋼 Pluteus salicinus is a psychedelic mushroom found in damp spots, growing and feeding exclusively on decaying wood. 
𓍊𓋼 Coloquially known as the Willow Shield mushroom.
𓍊𓋼 The cap is 5-8 cm in diameter, usually silvery-gray, and darker at the margins. The gills are crowded, white at first, then becoming pink-flesh colored. The edges discolor or bruise grayish. 
𓍊𓋼 The taste and odor is extremely unpleasant. 
𓍊𓋼 While not as potent as other psychedelics, it contains noticeable levels of psilocybin.
